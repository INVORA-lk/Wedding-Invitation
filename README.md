# Sri Lankan Royal Wedding Digital Invitation — Kavindu & Tharushi 🪷💍

An authentic, regal, and interactive Sri Lankan wedding invitation web application built with **React + Vite**, capturing the traditional Kandyan Poruwa rituals, golden lotus motifs (*Nelum Mala*), auspicious timing (*Nekatha*), and grand Colombo reception celebrations.

---

## 🇱🇰 Sri Lankan Wedding Experience & Features

1. **Auspicious Cultural Identity:**
   - **Traditional Greeting:** *ආයුබෝවන් (Ayubowan)* & *සුබ මංගලම් (Subha Mangalam)*.
   - **Triple Gem & Parental Blessings:** Honorific phrasing incorporating parents (*දෙගුරුන්ගේ ආශිර්වාදය*).
   - **Royal Color Palette:** Kandyan Gold, Deep Crimson Maroon (*රතු සහ රන්වන්*), Sacred Lotus Cream, and Emerald accents.
   - **Traditional Motifs:** Sacred blossoming Lotus (*Nelum Mala*) and intricate Sri Lankan *Liya Wela* scrollwork.
   - **Hero Couple Art:** Custom illustrated Sri Lankan couple in traditional Kandyan wedding attire (Maroon velvet embroidered Nilame jacket & bride in ivory Osariya with cascade floral bouquet).
   - **Closing Going-Away Art:** Beautiful watercolor couple walking away hand-in-hand (groom in black tuxedo & bride in flowing gown with veil), framed with romantic typography and blessings.

2. **Poruwa & Reception Venues (Colombo, Sri Lanka):**
   - **09:30 AM — Traditional Poruwa Ceremony & Magul Bera:**
     *The Grand Ballroom, Cinnamon Grand Colombo* (Auspicious Poruwa Ascent at **09:48 AM**).
   - **07:00 PM — Grand Reception & Baila Celebration:**
     *The Balmoral Hall, The Kingsbury Colombo*.
   - Interactive modal with embedded maps, Google Maps, and Waze direct navigation.

3. **Authentic Itinerary (චාරිත්‍ර පෙළගැස්ම):**
   - 09:30 AM: Magul Bera & Traditional Welcome with Jasmine garlands (*මඟුල් බෙර වාදනය*)
   - 09:48 AM: Auspicious Poruwa Ascent (*සුබ නැකතින් පෝරුවට ගොඩවීම*)
   - 10:15 AM: Lighting of the Traditional Brass Oil Lamp (*සාම්ප්‍රදායික පහන දැල්වීම*)
   - 11:30 AM: Traditional Kiribath & Sweetmeats Feast (*කිරිබත් සහ කැවුම් සංග්‍රහය*)
   - 07:30 PM: Evening Reception, Live Band & Baila Dancing (*සංගීතය සහ බයිලා නර්තනය*)
   - 12:00 AM: Going Away / Farewell of the Newlyweds (*යුවල නික්මයාම*)

4. **Interactive RSVP with Sri Lankan Elements:**
   - Attendance confirmation (Poruwa, Reception, or Both).
   - Sri Lankan dietary options: *Traditional Sri Lankan Wedding Buffet, Halal Certified, Pure Vegetarian, Jain Vegetarian, Western Gourmet*.
   - Live Band / DJ Baila song request field (*e.g., Kandy Lamissi, Yamu Yamu*).
   - One-click confirmation via WhatsApp with formatted message + celebratory confetti.

5. **Gifts & Blessings (සුබ පැතුම් සහ තිළිණ):**
   - Traditional card & blessing box details.
   - Bank transfer accounts for leading Sri Lankan banks (**Commercial Bank of Ceylon / Hatton National Bank** in LKR) with 1-click copy buttons.

6. **Ambiance & Audio Experience:**
   - **Classical Wedding Piano (Default):** Romantic concert grand piano playing Pachelbel's *Canon in D* with authentic string decay and concert hall reverb.
   - **Traditional Sri Lankan Flute:** Auspicious meditative wooden flute melody (*Mangala Geetha*) with warm Tanpura drone.
   - **Upload Custom Song:** Built-in audio selector popup allowing the couple to upload any MP3/audio track directly from their device.
   - **Floating Lotus & Jasmine Petals:** Ambient animation of golden lotus petals and fragrant jasmine blossoms drifting softly across the screen.
   - **Countdown Timer:** Live countdown to the auspicious Poruwa moment (*සුබ නැකත*) + Google Calendar and Apple/Outlook (.ics) export.
